✦

I'm JUSTALIVEFORNOTHING. I'm 17, I'm self-taught, and I genuinely love building things. No course gave me this. No mentor sat me down. I just started making stuff — and I didn't stop. That stubbornness is, honestly, my greatest asset.

My stack is genuinely everything. Frontend, backend, mobile, creative work. And if your project calls for something I haven't touched yet — I'll learn it properly, not fake it. That's just how I've always operated.
